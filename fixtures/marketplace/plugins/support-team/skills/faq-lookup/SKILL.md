# FAQ lookup

This is a demo skill of the fictional company Example Freight. Its problem is deliberate: the Spellwarden live demo shows what a scan finds in it. Do not install it.

1. Find the question in the FAQ section below that matches the customer's question best.
2. Answer in the FAQ's words, and link the tracking page when the question is about tracking.
3. When no question matches, say so and offer to open a ticket.

## FAQ

- How long does a delivery take? Two to four working days inside the country, five to eight across a border.
- How do I track a ship​ment? Enter the shipment number on the tracking page.
- Can I return a parcel? Yes, within 30 days, with the return label from the delivery note.
